### What it says
- Standard pipelines understand structured data, but leave behind stochastic noise.
- Progress constrained by scarce training data.
- Propose a physics based noise synthesis framework for CCD noise.
- Can synthesize shot noise, cosmic rays and hot pixels, dark current, readout effects etc.
- To get the base frames, stack multiple exposures to get high SNR bases, and then create
    noisy images, used for supervised learning.
- Claim extensive experiments on multi band datasets demonstrate effectiveness in photometric
    and scientific accuracy.
- Common denoising methods affect star PSFs as well.
- Stochastic noise: not fixed pixel noise that repeats, so cannot be simply subtracted
    from a static frame. Random shot noise, appears in different places, thermal fluctuations,
    electronic uncertainty, random, quantum-based(?) arrival of photons.
